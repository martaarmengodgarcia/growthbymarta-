## From launching Meta from scratch to improving demo conversion

When I joined the company, there were no active Meta campaigns.

The first opportunity I identified was retargeting people who had already visited the website but left without booking a demo.

I built the campaigns from scratch: creating different audiences based on the pages they had visited and their level of intent, developing ads for each group and excluding existing customers and contacts who had already converted.

At first, the ads sent users directly to the Book a Demo page.

When I reviewed the data, I saw that more than 60 people had reached the page through Meta, but **conversion was almost non-existent**.

We had managed to bring them back and regain their attention, but something was going wrong at the final step.

**What I did**

I wanted to understand whether those users simply did not have enough intent or whether the problem was the page itself.

To test this, I launched an A/B test:

- part of the traffic continued to the Book a Demo page;
- the rest used Meta's native lead forms to request a demo directly from the ad.

**The Lead Ads began converting significantly better.**

That led us to review the Book a Demo experience. The form was long and asked for too much information for an initial enquiry.

We redesigned the page and replaced the form with a shorter, progressive version.

The test did more than improve the Meta campaigns. It helped us **identify a conversion problem affecting everyone who reached the Book a Demo page**, including visitors coming from organic search, content and partners.

**Impact**

Over two months:

| Metric | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Contacts generated through Meta | 50+ |
| Opportunities created | 20+ |
| Improvement in Book a Demo conversion | +50% |
| New acquisition channel | Launched from scratch |

Meta became a new source of contacts and sales opportunities.

The changes to the Book a Demo page also increased conversion by more than 50%, improving the performance of traffic coming from other channels.

What started as a retargeting campaign ended up helping us improve one of the website's main conversion points.

The value of the project was not only in launching a new channel, but in **using campaign data to uncover a wider funnel problem** and improve the experience for every visitor.
